# Privacy Policy for Pilzy

**Last Updated: December 25, 2025**

At **Pilzy**, we believe that your health data is your own. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to your privacy and explains how we handle information within the Pilzy mobile application.

---

### 1. Data Privacy & Local-First Philosophy
Pilzy is designed as a **local-first** application. This means:
* **No Server Storage:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your medical data (medication names, dosages, or schedules) to any external servers.
* **On-Device Storage:** All information you input is stored exclusively on your device using a secure local database.
* **Your Control:** Since we do not have a central database, we cannot see, sell, or share your data with anyone.

### 2. Permissions Required
To function effectively as a medical reminder, Pilzy requests the following permissions:
* **Notifications:** To send you timely reminders for your medications and daily summary reports.
* **Exact Alarms (Android):** To ensure reminders trigger precisely at the scheduled time, even when the device is idle.
* **Haptic Feedback:** To provide tactile confirmation during app interactions.



### 3. Data Security
Because your data stays on your phone, its security depends on your device's security. We recommend using biometric authentication (FaceID/TouchID) or a strong passcode on your phone to ensure that your medication history remains private.

### 4. Third-Party Services
Pilzy does not include third-party advertising, tracking pixels, or analytics SDKs. We do not track your behavior or usage habits for marketing purposes.

### 5. Children’s Privacy
Pilzy does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. As no data is transmitted to us, we do not store information from any users, regardless of age.

### 6. Data Deletion
If you wish to delete all data associated with Pilzy, simply:
1. Clear the App Data in your device settings, or
2. Uninstall the application.
   This will permanently erase all local databases and history.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by a new "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
